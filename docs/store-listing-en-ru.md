**Description:**

```
Hi, Anxiety is a gentle weekly diary for self-observation — not a medical app and not emergency help.

The idea is simple: don't fight anxiety, get to know it. When you notice sleep, stress, medications, and habits, patterns appear. Patterns reduce uncertainty — and uncertainty is what anxiety feeds on.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK
• Medications — time, name, dose, taken status
• Flexible schedules — weekdays, intervals, monthly intake
• Reminders and stock tracking with refill alerts
• Sleep — night and day sleep, awakenings, night observation mode
• Triggers — from a catalog or your own notes
• Activity — walking, running, strength, and more
• Anxiety episodes — log episodes and review the week
• "Where am I today" — growth rings for actions and emotions
• Day notes and search across your entries

WEEKLY VIEW
See the whole week at a glance. Flip between weeks, pick a month, and on Sunday review summaries and charts. Export a weekly PDF to share with your doctor or therapist — only when you choose.

PANIC ATTACK HELP
During an episode, open quick grounding steps in the app and log what happened. Includes contraindications for cold-water techniques.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No account required
• Diary data stays on your device
• No ads, no tracking
• 10 interface languages

IMPORTANT
Hi, Anxiety is a personal diary tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care. If you feel unwell or are in crisis, seek professional help immediately.

Support: hi.anxiety.support@gmail.com
Privacy: https://dekira9.github.io/hi-anxiety-legal/
```
